# Safety, lawful operation and disclaimer

**USE AT YOUR OWN RISK. THIS IS EXPERIMENTAL FIRMWARE.**

Installing, configuring or using ClearUI can render your radio unusable
("brick" it), erase settings or calibration, damage equipment, or cause
unexpected transmissions and interference. Recovery is not guaranteed.
Back up configuration and calibration before flashing, verify the exact
hardware model, and retain a known-good recovery image. Do not rely on this
experimental firmware for emergency, life-safety or other critical communications.

## Operator responsibility

You are responsible for checking and following all laws and rules applicable
to your location, equipment and radio service, and for obtaining any required
licenses or authorizations **before transmitting**. Check permitted frequencies,
power, bandwidth, operating modes, identification and equipment-approval requirements.

Software capability, an unlocked frequency range, a programmed channel or an
amateur-radio license is not blanket permission to transmit on any frequency.
Do not transmit without the required authorization or cause unlawful interference.
The project does not grant a radio license or certify equipment compliance.
Receive-only flags and other software safeguards can contain bugs; do not rely
on them as the sole means of preventing unauthorized transmission.
